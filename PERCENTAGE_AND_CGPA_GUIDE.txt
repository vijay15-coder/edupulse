PERCENTAGE FORMULA AND CGPA FORMULA - COMPLETE GUIDE
======================================================

This document explains how to calculate percentage and CGPA, with formulas, examples, and implementation details.


===========================================
1) PERCENTAGE FORMULA
===========================================

A. SIMPLE PERCENTAGE FORMULA
-----------------------------

Formula:
    Percentage = (Obtained Marks / Total Marks) × 100

Example 1 (Single Subject):
- Student scored: 75 out of 100
- Percentage = (75 / 100) × 100 = 75%

Example 2 (Multiple Subjects - Weighted Average):
- Subject 1: Scored 80 out of 100 (Weight: 30%)
- Subject 2: Scored 70 out of 100 (Weight: 40%)
- Subject 3: Scored 90 out of 100 (Weight: 30%)

Weighted Percentage = (80 × 0.30) + (70 × 0.40) + (90 × 0.30)
                    = 24 + 28 + 27
                    = 79%

B. CUMULATIVE PERCENTAGE (Overall Score)
-----------------------------------------

Formula:
    Total Percentage = (Total Marks Obtained / Total Maximum Marks) × 100

Example:
- Total marks obtained across all subjects: 345
- Total maximum marks across all subjects: 500

Total Percentage = (345 / 500) × 100 = 69%

C. SEMESTER/YEAR PERCENTAGE
----------------------------

Formula:
    Semester Percentage = (Total Marks in Semester / Total Possible Marks in Semester) × 100

Example (Semester with 5 subjects):
- Subject 1: 80/100
- Subject 2: 75/100
- Subject 3: 85/100
- Subject 4: 90/100
- Subject 5: 70/100

Total = 80 + 75 + 85 + 90 + 70 = 400
Maximum = 100 × 5 = 500

Semester Percentage = (400 / 500) × 100 = 80%


===========================================
2) CGPA FORMULA (CUMULATIVE GRADE POINT AVERAGE)
===========================================

A. 10-POINT CGPA SCALE
----------------------

Grade Point Scale (Most Common in India):
- A+: 10 points (90-100%)
- A:  9 points (80-89%)
- B+: 8 points (70-79%)
- B:  7 points (60-69%)
- C+: 6 points (50-59%)
- C:  5 points (40-49%)
- D:  4 points (Below 40%)
- F:  0 points (Failed/Below threshold)

Formula:
    CGPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Σ (Total Credit Hours)

Example (Semester with 5 subjects):
- Subject 1: Grade A+ (10), Credit Hours: 4
- Subject 2: Grade A (9), Credit Hours: 3
- Subject 3: Grade B+ (8), Credit Hours: 4
- Subject 4: Grade A (9), Credit Hours: 3
- Subject 5: Grade B (7), Credit Hours: 2

Calculation:
Step 1: Multiply each grade point by its credit hours:
  S1: 10 × 4 = 40
  S2: 9 × 3 = 27
  S3: 8 × 4 = 32
  S4: 9 × 3 = 27
  S5: 7 × 2 = 14

Step 2: Sum all weighted grades:
  Total Weighted Points = 40 + 27 + 32 + 27 + 14 = 140

Step 3: Sum all credit hours:
  Total Credit Hours = 4 + 3 + 4 + 3 + 2 = 16

Step 4: Calculate CGPA:
  CGPA = 140 / 16 = 8.75 out of 10


B. 4-POINT CGPA SCALE
---------------------

Grade Point Scale (Used in USA, Canada, etc.):
- A:  4.0 points (90-100%)
- A-: 3.7 points (87-89%)
- B+: 3.3 points (83-86%)
- B:  3.0 points (80-82%)
- B-: 2.7 points (77-79%)
- C+: 2.3 points (73-76%)
- C:  2.0 points (70-72%)
- C-: 1.7 points (67-69%)
- D+: 1.3 points (63-66%)
- D:  1.0 points (60-62%)
- F:  0.0 points (Below 60%)

Formula: SAME AS 10-POINT
    CGPA = Σ (Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Σ (Total Credit Hours)

Example (Semester with 3 subjects):
- Subject 1: Grade A (4.0), Credit Hours: 3
- Subject 2: Grade B+ (3.3), Credit Hours: 4
- Subject 3: Grade B (3.0), Credit Hours: 3

Calculation:
Step 1: Multiply each grade point by its credit hours:
  S1: 4.0 × 3 = 12.0
  S2: 3.3 × 4 = 13.2
  S3: 3.0 × 3 = 9.0

Step 2: Sum all weighted grades:
  Total Weighted Points = 12.0 + 13.2 + 9.0 = 34.2

Step 3: Sum all credit hours:
  Total Credit Hours = 3 + 4 + 3 = 10

Step 4: Calculate CGPA:
  CGPA = 34.2 / 10 = 3.42 out of 4.0


C. CONVERTING PERCENTAGE TO CGPA (10-POINT SCALE)
---------------------------------------------------

If you have percentage and want CGPA:

Simple Linear Conversion:
    CGPA = Percentage / 10

Example:
- Percentage: 75%
- CGPA (10-point) = 75 / 10 = 7.5

Formula with Grade Brackets:
If Percentage >= 90: CGPA = 10
If Percentage >= 80: CGPA = 9
If Percentage >= 70: CGPA = 8
If Percentage >= 60: CGPA = 7
If Percentage >= 50: CGPA = 6
If Percentage >= 40: CGPA = 5
If Percentage < 40:  CGPA = 0


D. CONVERTING PERCENTAGE TO CGPA (4-POINT SCALE)
--------------------------------------------------

Simple Linear Conversion:
    CGPA = (Percentage / 100) × 4

Example:
- Percentage: 85%
- CGPA (4-point) = (85 / 100) × 4 = 3.4

Formula with Grade Brackets:
If Percentage >= 90: CGPA = 4.0
If Percentage >= 85: CGPA = 3.7
If Percentage >= 80: CGPA = 3.3
If Percentage >= 75: CGPA = 3.0
... and so on


E. OVERALL/CUMULATIVE CGPA (Across Multiple Semesters)
-------------------------------------------------------

Formula:
    Overall CGPA = Σ (Semester CGPA × Semester Credits) / Σ (Total Semester Credits)

Example (3 Semesters):
- Semester 1: CGPA 7.8, Total Credits 16
- Semester 2: CGPA 8.2, Total Credits 16
- Semester 3: CGPA 7.9, Total Credits 16

Calculation:
Step 1: Multiply each semester CGPA by its credits:
  S1: 7.8 × 16 = 124.8
  S2: 8.2 × 16 = 131.2
  S3: 7.9 × 16 = 126.4

Step 2: Sum all weighted semester GPA:
  Total = 124.8 + 131.2 + 126.4 = 382.4

Step 3: Sum all semester credits:
  Total Credits = 16 + 16 + 16 = 48

Step 4: Calculate Overall CGPA:
  Overall CGPA = 382.4 / 48 = 7.967 ≈ 8.0


===========================================
3) IMPLEMENTATION IN EDUPULSE
===========================================

A. SELECTING CGPA FORMAT DURING COLLEGE ONBOARDING
---------------------------------------------------

When SuperAdmin creates a college, they select:
1. CGPA Format: 10-POINT | 4-POINT | PERCENTAGE
2. Grade mapping is stored in academic_settings.cgpa_format

This setting controls how grades are calculated and displayed throughout the college's portal.


B. CALCULATION HELPER FUNCTIONS
--------------------------------

The system uses calculation utilities available in:
- lib/calculations.ts (frontend)
- backend/src/utils/calculations.ts (server-side)

Available functions:

1. calculatePercentage(obtainedMarks, totalMarks)
   Returns: percentage as number

2. convertPercentageToCGPA10(percentage)
   Returns: CGPA on 10-point scale

3. convertPercentageToCGPA4(percentage)
   Returns: CGPA on 4-point scale

4. calculateCGPAFromGrades(gradePointsWithCredits)
   Input: [{gradePoints: 10, creditHours: 4}, ...]
   Returns: calculated CGPA

5. calculateOverallCGPA(semesterCGPAsWithCredits)
   Input: [{cgpa: 8.5, credits: 16}, ...]
   Returns: overall CGPA


C. STORING AND RETRIEVING CALCULATIONS
---------------------------------------

When a student's marks are entered:
1. Percentage is calculated immediately
2. Grade is assigned based on percentage + CGPA format
3. CGPA is calculated from grades and credit hours
4. Results are stored in marks table
5. Displayed in student's results/grades page

===========================================
5) COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
===========================================

1. Forgetting to divide by 100 when converting percentage to decimal
   ❌ Wrong: CGPA = percentage × 4
   ✅ Right: CGPA = (percentage / 100) × 4

2. Not using credit hours in CGPA calculation
   ❌ Wrong: CGPA = Sum of grades / number of subjects
   ✅ Right: CGPA = Sum(grade × credits) / Sum(all credits)

3. Mixing percentage and CGPA scales without conversion
   ❌ Don't compare 75% (percentage) with 3.5 (4-point CGPA) directly
   ✅ Convert both to same scale first

4. Forgetting attendance threshold multiplier
   Some colleges reduce percentage if attendance is below minimum
   ✅ Formula: Final % = Percentage × (attendance% / 100) if attendance < threshold

5. Not accounting for pass/fail conditions
   ✅ Always check if marks >= 40% (or college minimum) before awarding grade
